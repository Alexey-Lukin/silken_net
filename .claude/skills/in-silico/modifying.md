# In-silico — when modifying (the bodies)

> **Auxiliary file of the `in-silico` skill. Read this before editing a script, a shared constant, a
> cache or a doc that quotes one.** The one-line index in `SKILL.md` §When Modifying is the carrier;
> this file is the mechanism, the incident that bought it, and its bounds. Edit rules HERE — the
> index is generated (`ruby scripts/guard_craft_index.rb --write`).
>
> **Numbered on 2026-09-18, append-only from then on.** Before the split these were unnumbered
> bullets, so older citations name the section (`§When Modifying`) and never a number; the numbers
> follow the pre-split order.

1. **Adding a ligand is a five-step chain, and skipping the last step leaves downstream MD stale**: script 0N (parameterize) → add to gaff_cache → SDF to ligands/ → add test → rerun downstream MD

2. **Change a constant ONLY in `tools/in_silico/lib/constants.py`, then find every cached JSON that used it.**

3. **After any change run the in-silico tests, commit, then update every SSOT doc that quotes the result**: `pytest tools/in_silico/tests/` → commit → update the SSOT docs listed at the top of `SKILL.md`

4. **Drift-proof comments/constants — never hardcode a mirror of another script's result; LOAD it from that script's cache JSON at runtime** (crude t_ij, single-snapshot β·d, an E°; 24b/28b do this) so it can't silently diverge. A **DERIVED aggregate (a Hammett LFER slope / fit) must ALSO be computed INTO the cache** (21e writes a `lfer` block), else cache-vs-doc tooling has nothing to diff against; and **read the canonical fit-set from the GENERATOR** — `60_paper_figures.py` excludes cf3 → slope −0.92, r²=1.00; blindly including cf3 gives −0.90 (a trap «тільки ретельно» caught)

5. **A cache PROSE field (`verdict`, notes) is what docs quote — build every factual clause from the computed data, never type it.** `55` printed a derived fatigue answer while its cache `verdict` still said «NOT over the SF-2 line» and «on the PRINTED branch not one of the six cleared it» for a day after the inputs moved — the numbers in the same JSON said 6 of 6 and 4 of 6 (fixed 2026-09-13, `00_07` HW.34). A re-derived console report does not re-derive the cache string; grep the cache's prose for every count and comparison it states. ⊕ **A verdict FLAG must test every criterion its verdict sentence prints, or split into one flag per criterion:** `51` cached and printed a per-row `safe` that judged hoop stress only, beside «H7/s6 safe across −30 to +40 °C», while its own +40 °C row had lost interference at the band minimum (split 2026-09-14 into `hoop_below_peek_yield` + `interference_retained_at_band_min`).

6. **Its numeric twin: a cache NUMBER rounded for display is OUTPUT — never compute FROM that row.** `55` stored EI and radial play to four decimals and endurance to one, then computed its seam, edge and wear blocks from those rows (EI +0.6 %). One quantity carried two values in one cache (span optimism 41.3 ⊥ 40.8 %), and the rounding manufactured «fails at both band ends — the seam conclusion is robust» in SUMMARY and `00_07` HW.34; the exact member gives k 0.499 against the 0.50 marker, a tie (fixed 2026-09-14, `branch_member()`; the k itself was retired later that day with the drift picture, but the rounding lesson stands). Take the member from its definition; pin that two owners of one quantity agree; before calling a verdict on a third-decimal margin robust, re-derive it at the precision it was born with. ⚠️ The LOAD-from-cache rule above inherits whatever precision the writer stored — ask what that was before computing from it.

7. **A contact station read off the FREE shape at FULL load is not an equilibrium.** Under a tip load the deflection grows monotonically from the clamp, so the first station to reach a uniform wall is the FARTHEST one, and from then on the load is reacted there. `55` read its station where the full-load free shape crosses the play, and several ratified verdicts stood on that reading until `68` solved the contact (2026-09-14, `00_07` HW.34). Before quoting a contact station, ask whether the shape it came from is still admissible once the contact exists. ✅ `55`'s contact blocks were re-derived on the same solver the same day (`tools/in_silico/lib/beam_contact.py`, one home — `68` imports `55`, so the solver could live in neither script). ⊕ What the re-derivation bought beyond the station, and it is the same trap one level down — and then one level further: once touched down, a rod whose exit is PRESCRIBED by a rigid wall keeps one shape whatever the load (in-contact sliding 0), so the `r·θ` slip the drift block took from the free slope at the station was called a CEILING for an afternoon — and it is not one either: the real wall is the polymer on the Ti bore's edge, it yields, and the rod rotates in contact up to the Ti-bore stop. The cache carries the BRACKET in fields (`sliding_in_contact_um_rigid_kinematics: 0.0` ⊥ `sliding_in_contact_per_cycle_um_upper`), the rigid rotation demoted to `out_of_contact_rotation_per_cycle_um_rigid_wall`, and every rigid-wall figure carries its SIGN (`coaxial_root_bound: LOWER…`, `reaction_bound: UPPER…`). 🔑 **A bound quoted without its sign becomes a nominal** (the coordinator's catch, 2026-09-14): the same rigid-wall root stress went out as «capped at 8.04 MPa» while it is the LOWER end of [8.04, 56.3] — and the sign flipped a finding, not a digit: the wear budget re-earns the rejection of the conformal films only against a rigid wall, because on the bracket the in-contact sliding scales with the polymer's own wall. A quantity read off a shape must be re-asked once the shape is constrained — and once the constraint is found to be soft.

8. **A control that cannot fail is not a control — say in the docstring what each assert CAN and CANNOT catch.** `68` first shipped an equilibrium assert that is an identity of the stiffness matrix's rigid-body null space (it passes with EI doubled or the mouth moved) and a mesh check on rows whose contacts sat on nodes, where Hermite elements are exact — an adversarial review found both. What caught real defects were controls with an INDEPENDENT formula or a location the solve does not enforce: closed forms at the pad plane and at the mouth, and the wall tested at element MIDPOINTS, which caught a uniform mesh leaving the mouth between nodes on a new geometry (on the old one the mouth had sat on a node by luck). ⊕ Same lesson, other direction: `68` also carried «in every configuration computed here the root is the peak-moment section» as PROSE in its cache; read from the moment field of every solve (2026-09-14) it was false on two lock-window classes — a 9 mrad tilt about the mouth and the pogo couple at the exit. A claim in a cache string is a claim without a measurer until a field carries it, and this one was about to become the ground of a canon correction.

9. **A solver loop needs an explicit non-convergence branch (`for … else: raise`, plus a cycle detector on an active set), and its tolerances come from the MEASURED solve precision.** A scratchpad solver with an iteration cap and no `else` could hand back a non-converged state as a number; the committed one refused on a cycle, which exposed a violation tolerance below the solve's own precision re-picking an already-active node. Measure the precision first (in `68` the relative clamp-moment error is ~4e-7 on the 23 mm rod and ~2e-6 on the 38–39 mm rods) and write that number beside the tolerance.

10. **A literature constant that does not EXIST at your conditions is not a number to pick — bound it by SIGN, then price it.** `67` (HW.3) needed the calcium and magnesium malate complexes at I = 0; no open primary has them, only APPARENT constants from another medium, and electing one would have rested the whole admissible window on it. The sign needs no value: a calcium ligand left out can only RAISE the calcium-oxalate SI, a magnesium ligand can at most free what magnesium holds — so a HARD BOUND (those ligands off, magnesium oxalate-inert, the documented constants at their SI-raising ends) carries the window, its dominance over every documented reading is ASSERTED at every corner, the readings PRICE the gap instead of hiding it, and the verdict side inverts into a break-even constant. Same family as `55`'s `WELD_KNOCKDOWN_MEASURED = None`. ⚠️ **A bound is a bound for the ONE quantity it was built for:** the same scenario, magnesium made oxalate-inert by construction, reported magnesium oxalate SUPERSATURATED until it was barred from reporting magnesium solids — every other number a bounding scenario prints is an artefact of the switch you threw. 🔴 **And follow the sign through EVERY channel the constant acts on — the first draft argued by mass action alone.** A neutral magnesium complex also takes two divalent ions out of solution, raises the activity coefficients of Ca²⁺ and ox²⁻ and so raises the SI; an adversarial review caught it with the "safe" window already in canon. The fix was structural, not a caveat: the bound now takes that channel's limit (every magnesium ion sequestered), and dominance is asserted against each missing constant SWEPT to strong values — a documented reading is not the edge of what the constant could be, so an assert against readings alone could never have seen it.

11. **Two primary readings that DISAGREE are a bracket of NAMED readings, never a pick — and a consumer's ceiling is the ADVERSE reading, cited.** `62` (HW.43, 2026-09-14) needed the sway frequency of *Pinus sylvestris*: the field literature gives 0.26–0.312 Hz (Nickl 2022 · Kolbe & Schindler 2021) and 0.74 Hz (Schindler & Kolbe 2020 — one tree, one January day), and neither source names the cause of the gap. Electing either would have hidden a factor of two to three inside a «budget». So `tools/in_silico/lib/constants.py` holds BOTH (`SWAY_F0_HZ_LOW_READING` ⊥ `SWAY_F0_HZ_HIGH_READING`), the cache carries `readings_ratio_x` and `cause_named_by_sources: false`, and every consumer (`59`, `55`, the S-N test plan and its RFQ) takes its ceiling at the adverse reading WITH the citation, the other reading printed beside it as the price of that choice. Taking the convenient reading silently picks a side, and nothing downstream can see it. ⚠️ **Before a RANGE becomes an input, ask which WORLD measured it:** the retired single budget came from a 1–5 Hz band that is a BENCH range, faster than either field reading — a test-rig figure had been standing in for the tree.

12. 🔴 **When a VERDICT replaces the canon premise a script priced, the script does not become stale — its premise does, and the two need different treatment** (`67`, HW.3, 2026-09-17). Script 67 measured that every corner of the canon sap RANGES is supersaturated; the founder then replaced the ranges with a POINT. What was done, and what the shape of it is: Q1–Q5 keep running on the pre-verdict ranges (they are the GROUND of the verdict — delete them and the decision loses its reason), the ratified point enters as a NEW question (`Q6`) with its own cache block, and the doc↔cache pins MOVE from the range ends to the point. The comment on the premise constant is rewritten to say it is pre-verdict, because a constant labelled «the canon recipe» after canon changed is the quiet lie. ⛔ Do not re-run Q1–Q5 «for the new recipe»: a point has no corners, and the verdict's evidence is the corner scan that existed. 🔑 Portable: **a superseded premise is history, not drift — keep it, date it, and pin the new decision separately.**

13. 🔴 **Applying a ratified NOMINAL is a MODEL change, not a key swap — and it can change WHICH extreme is binding** (`55`, HW.34, 2026-09-18). With the bore at wire − 11.41 µm the channel play depends on WHO carries the interference: bore-carried → the tube's free OD shrinks first and the play grows across the window; wire-carried (wire over nominal, bore at nominal) → the wire's excess swells the OD with no bore shrink to pay for it, and the play falls below its zero-interference value. The first application published only the bore-carried extreme and called the zero-interference play «the lower end» — an adversarial read caught it. Publish BOTH extremes, re-read every sync phrase that states a direction, and re-run every importer (`68`).

14. 🔴 **An input nudged to keep a model well-formed is a HYPOTHESIS about configuration — and it travels as a CLAIM on the output** (`55`, HW.34, 2026-09-11). At Ø1.30 the canon liner `0.150` left zero play and the regime degenerated, so the row carried `0.1475`; the instrument then described a rod-side allocation nobody ratified and fed canon «140 → ~2.5 µm» (measured properly: 6.8×). Nothing flags it — the nudge is locally defensible and the output is read as measurement. The tell: a value that differs from its canon twin by a rounding-sized amount with no verdict behind it. **Write the nudge where the RESULT is read, or remove the need for it.**

15. **When a swept coefficient becomes the operating point, diff the cache field by field: the numbers must move linearly and boringly, and only the FLAGS may be interesting** (`55`, 2026-09-18: `ENDURANCE_OVER_YIELD` 0.45 → 0.40 scaled every SF ×0.889 and flipped exactly three membership flags). Anything else means the re-run changed more than the coefficient.
