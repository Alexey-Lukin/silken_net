0. 🔴 **`conda env list` in a non-interactive shell answers about ITSELF, not about the machine — ask the FILESYSTEM.** The `conda` shell function is only defined by an interactive init, so a tool-driven Bash gets a stub that reports just `base`; reading that as «the env is not installed here» is a measurement substitution. **Roster:** `ls ~/miniforge3/envs/`. **Run a script without activation:** `~/miniforge3/envs/silken_md/bin/python scripts/NN_*.py` (same for `silken_lint`, `silken_ml`, `pymol_tmp`). (Proving that a cache regeneration changed only what you meant → §When Modifying #15.)

1. **Shared lib is SSOT — every SHARED or canon-mirrored constant, every cache root and `banner()` come from `lib/` and are never redefined locally.** A coefficient private to one script's model lives in that script, named and cited there (`55`'s `ENDURANCE_OVER_YIELD`); where to change which kind → §When Modifying #2.

2. **A SMILES fix cascades — fixing one in 02-08 means rerunning ALL downstream MD that uses that ligand** (check the GAFF cache too; the ligand → MD edges are the graph in `SKILL.md`).

3. **DFT: NEVER two heavy jobs on same CPU** — cache thrashing makes both 2× slower (observed: a 92-min solo job took 190+ min alongside another). Small molecules (H₂O, lumiflavin) OK to parallelize; MD-on-GPU alongside a CPU DFT job is fine (different hardware) — the hazard is two CPU-bound DFT jobs sharing cache. ⊕ **A parallel pool of ANY CPU-bound job counts as the second heavy job** [`92`, 2026-10-14]: a 5-worker pool of contact solves (10 BLAS threads) was launched beside a running `21e` geometry optimisation in the `silken_net-acceptors` worktree (≈ 9 cores) and took the machine — caught only by reading `ps` after the launch. 🔑 Before launching a pool or a long run, `ps -A -o pid,%cpu,etime,command | grep python` first; with a DFT running, keep the new job to the cores it leaves (here 2 workers × 2 threads), and never kill by a pattern that could match the other job's processes.

4. **No density_fit() for Os/Ce — the auto-generated aux basis for heavy metals is 3× SLOWER than standard integrals.**

5. **Open-shell transition metals can oscillate forever — `level_shift=0.3` stops UKS on Os(III), Co-Ce and Ce; it steers the PATH an SCF takes (hence possibly the state it lands on), not the orbital energies a converged run reports, so switch it on only where the SCF oscillates.** Without it the oscillation ran 60h+. `lib.dft_utils.dft_singlepoint` keeps the shift OFF by default (`level_shift_open=0.0`) because the committed B3LYP Os rows were converged so, and rescues a non-converged SCF with SOSCF (`mf.newton()`). 📌 **Дописка 2026-10-11 (the ground above is NOT the one this rule stood on until then):** the earlier ground — «the shift biases the reported LUMO, so read E_total, not LUMO» — is empty for a converged run: PySCF re-diagonalises the unshifted Fock matrix once converged (the SCF kernel's `conv_check` step) and the Newton solver canonicalises with the shift at 0 — read in the source of 2.11.0 (recorded) and 2.13.1 (lock); on H₂O⁺ a 0.3 E_h shift moved the converged LUMO by 0.5 meV, where a biased one would move it by 8.2 eV. The candidate was an adversary's (`00_07` HW.5.IS, 2026-10-09); the default survives on the other ground, the committed path. It also struck a clause from paper Methods §2.2 («(shift-biased) virtual orbital energies»). ⚠️ At the ωB97X tiers (21f's, shift 0.3 on Os(III)) the shift did NOT converge four of five Os(III) points in 100 cycles — the rescue did (the 21e ωB97X run logs) — and «converges» means a STATIONARY POINT, not the ground state → #14.

6. **MD goes NaN at the NVT ramp unless it pre-relaxes at 10K and ramps from 50K in 10K steps** — 10K pre-relaxation (1000 steps at 10K) + start NVT from 50K + ramp step 10K (not 5K); without it about half the multi-ligand runs NaN'd, worst at high T (313K). Use `maxIterations=10000` for minimization. ⚠️ **Scope: the production pattern of `12`/`14`/`15`** (`13` and `16` pre-relax at 10 K without a ramp; `10` and `11` predate the pattern and ramp from 100 K) — **NOT the CI smoke gate**, whose iteration cap #8 forbids raising.

7. **MD trajectories are gitignored — commit only the JSON/PNG summaries** under `cache/`; `cache/runs/` (trajectories, checkpoints) and the MD binary extensions are ignored by `tools/in_silico/.gitignore`.

8. **The CI smoke MD went NaN from BOX SIZE, not RNG — shrink the box (drop disordered tails), never just re-run or raise the iteration cap.** 🔴 Seeding is not the fix: with `seed=42` on BOTH the velocity draw and the Langevin thermostat it NaN'd anyway (run 34261017635, 2026-09-08) — seeding makes a run reproducible GIVEN the positions and cannot un-clash them. ⛔ Don't raise the minimisation cap without measuring: on the oversized box the capped minimisation alone ate most of the 40-min budget, so a higher cap trades a NaN for a timeout. The measurement and the fix (the disordered N-terminal tail dropped before solvation) live in the docstring of `01_smoke_test_water_box.py`. Verify a touch locally with the exact CI env: `SILKEN_FORCE_PLATFORM=CPU SILKEN_WATER_PADDING=0.5 SILKEN_MIN_ITERATIONS=500 SILKEN_MD_STEPS=100`.

9. 🔴 **Declaring an anchor dimension: name the REFERENT, not the number — and if canon gives a RANGE, say which end you took and why.** No VALUE gate binds these scripts' literal constants to canon geometry: a pin was measured and refused (`00_07` HW.45, founder 2026-09-09), because a copy taken from a different referent carries a TRUE canon number — it breaks the BINDING, not the equality, so every pin form is green on it (ground: `00_06 §2`). The carriers are this rule and `picogk` gotcha 0a; why a gate is blind here — `guard-craft` #155. **Live instance:** `54`'s `L_ANODE_GYROID` takes the LOWER end of the canon gyroid-length range while the CEM mirror and sibling `58` take a value inside it. 🔑 **If the number is not an END, say what it IS — a worked example · a design point · a vendor figure — and whose,** and read the CLAUSE you cite, not just its address: a design point mirrored as a floor once sat in both machine halves while both had checked only the address (`52`'s `RF_ANT_TI_CLEARANCE_MIN`, now labelled OUR working floor beside the canon one). 🔴 **The referent can be wrong by AXIS while both ends are quoted correctly** — `50` compared the lattice with wood's LONGITUDINAL modulus for a part canon loads TRANSVERSELY (`01_01 §5.1`). ⚠️ A correction that lives only in a docstring and a `print()` has no machine reader — no cache field, no `CHECKS` row — so nothing reds when it rots. 🔴 **And the MIRROR of the same rule: our OWN threshold quoted as if it were an external norm.** `55`'s `INFINITE_LIFE_SF` is declared there as «the SF line this script calls infinite life», yet the tracker cited it as the bar an alloy must clear — a house constant borrowing the authority of a standard. Same axis, opposite direction: the rule above catches a canon range flattened into a floor; this catches a house constant inflated into a standard. **Practice: when a verdict turns on a threshold, say WHOSE it is in the same sentence — ours, the vendor’s, or the standard’s (and then name the standard).** Applied BEFORE the fact it looks like `55`'s weld block: the missing input stays VISIBLY missing (`WELD_KNOCKDOWN_MEASURED = None`), and both markers a vendor's value will be read against (`AS_PRINTED_DERATE` · `WROUGHT_DERATE`) are labelled OURS. 🔴 **A script's PROSE can name a quantity it never COMPUTES, and the prose is what travels** — a grep for the verdict's subject finds it richly discussed and the reader concludes the model answers it (wear in `55`, before any wear block existed). 🔑 Reflex for these scripts, whose verdict strings ride into the cache and from there into canon and the tracker: **a `verdict`/`caveats` string may state what the script MEASURED and what it DID NOT — and when it names the second, it must name it as absent, not merely discuss it.** The cheapest check before quoting a conclusion onward: grep the script for the quantity and ask whether the hits are assignments or sentences. 🔴 **A constant that declares its own subject OPEN keeps saying so after the subject is ratified** — the referent and the value can both be right and the MODALITY wrong, and downstream a soft flag reads as «this may still move». The ratification lands in CANON, never in the script, so nothing here reds: grep your constants for `ASSUMED`/`is_assumed`/«open ⚖️» after any verdict in their domain, and the `question`/`verdict`/`_note` strings for «open · not specified · assumed» too — a verdict answers the QUESTION before it moves a NUMBER. ⊕ **A derived figure has no lexical link to its cause, so the perimeter of a span fix is «what else is derived from this span», never a grep** — and a range quoted from a derived table takes both ends from the SAME row set (a bare-column minimum beside a bonded-column maximum is a row-mix). ⊕ **The END can be picked by a CLI DEFAULT — and then nobody picked it** (`00_07` HW.9 · HW.33, 2026-09-29): `tools/firmware/board_area_budget.rb` defaulted `--piezo murata`, the SMALLEST of three live candidates while the choice is open (HW.30), and printed the contour target without naming it, so canon and five tracker legs carried «Ø ≈ 21.1» as THE target while Mallory gives 21.9 / 22.5. 🔑 **A tool whose output feeds canon prints every input it substituted for an open choice and sweeps that choice beside the headline** — the calculator now does both, and its `--assert` pins each variant canon quotes.

**Practice:** every geometric constant cites either a `tools/cad/cem/*.json` field (canon-gated by `cem_canon_sync`) or a canon row; prefer importing from `tools/in_silico/lib/constants.py` over a local literal — the `D_BUS` divergence of this class was closed by ONE HOME (`D_BUS_ROD_MM`), not by a gate. A deliberately-off value carries its caveat in **every** carrier — docstring · the comment over the constant · the `caveats`/`verdict` string that rides into the cache · its `PIPELINE_STATUS` row · its `SUMMARY` section; sweep by OCCURRENCE, never by FILE. 🔴 **A constant that decomposes itself in prose reads as derived and is not** — when a span is a sum, read its terms from the manifests at runtime (as `55` does), and quote a term that has no manifest field AS a by-value crossing rather than promoting it into a CEM field, which would canonise a placeholder. A span constant is not a digit: it is the configuration every verdict in the file is about. **A categorical label derived from a sweep must say which quantifier it uses — ALL, SOME or NONE — or its else-branch will pick one for you.** **A descriptive adjective in a script comment is a claim about the part, and it needs a source exactly like a number does** («blind» for a channel the CAD cuts THROUGH changes the machining class).

10. 🔴 **A literature constant can be the right NUMBER in the wrong ROLE and of the wrong FORM — and the two errors compound silently** (`00_07` HW.5.IS). `J_MAX_25C` once held the NATIVE enzyme's current density at an OPERATING POINT where `30` needs the Michaelis-Menten ASYMPTOTE of the form we build, so under the source's own conditions the model returned half of what it was handed. The re-anchored value, its derivation and its error propagation live in the comment over `J_MAX_25C` in `tools/in_silico/lib/constants.py`. 🔑 **Three things worth carrying beyond this constant.** (a) **The route that looks authoritative can be the one that does not close:** an ABSOLUTE current from a table whose electrode area the paper never states needs a number nobody published — use it as a CONTROL instead. (b) **Propagate the uncertainty, never type it** — from the source's own error bars, and note which term dominates. (c) **Pin the RELATION, not the literals:** a derived pair moves together, so `test_cache_integrity` asserts the derivation and reds when one side moves alone. ⚖️ **The model still runs on the LAB CEILING** (the source's pH, electrode and flow, not our sap): ratified 2026-09-18 — the ceiling stays the model and the pH correction is printed BESIDE it as a bracket (`30`, from `PH_KINETICS_SYGMUND`), never folded in. Verify any correction factor against the primary BEFORE applying it, even when your own tracker carries a number — here the ratio turned out [S]-dependent, not a constant. ⛔ The bracket is an INDICATION for our electrode, not its measurement (free enzyme, small-molecule acceptor, another temperature), and the script says so in its own output. A re-anchor re-runs every consumer on the L4 line of the graph in `SKILL.md`, with the field diff of §When Modifying #15. 🔴 **The same error on a number that is OURS, and it is the easier one to make: a value introduced explicitly as a generous BOUND for question A, read as a DATUM for question B** (`00_07` HW.33, 2026-10-01). `52 §rim_datum_creep` (the rim datum in a DIMENSIONAL frame: it derives the initial bayonet clamp the rim needs for a given lift force) puts a "deliberately generous 100 N" (`GENEROUS_ORING_BOUND_N` in lib) on the unmeasured O-ring load — while its own `missing_datum` field says that load has no home in canon. Sizing the bayonet collar wall against that 100 N would have read a bound as a measurement, i.e. the FOLLOW-BACK class of `00_01 §1.1` with a number we wrote ourselves. **The tell is in the SOURCE's own wording, not in the number: "generous" · "even a …" · "an upper bound" · a `missing_datum` sitting in the same dict.** Reflex: before reusing a neighbouring model's number, read WHAT QUESTION it was introduced to answer — a bound that is conservative for A can be arbitrary for B, and inversion (ask at WHAT value your member becomes binding) needs no datum at all. ⊕ **But inversion returns the datum through the back door the moment its answer is COMPARED with that same bound** (`88`, 2026-10-13): `73` inverted correctly — the lug root binds at 457 N — and then wrote «the missing datum stops being a blocker because the generous bound is covered», which holds only if 100 N is an upper bound; Parker's band for our ring passes it below the nominal squeeze. The binding value is the answer; to call it safe, the number on the other side must be a SOURCED upper bound, not the bound you introduced for another question.

11. 🔴 **A part that enters a stack budget is judged on EVERY axis the envelope constrains, not on the axis the script was built for** (`00_07` HW.9, 2026-09-25). `52_z_stack_tolerance` judged the LoRa-E5 module by HEIGHT (2.5 mm, `RF_DECK_TALLEST_BOM_PART_MM`) for weeks, while its 12 × 12 mm footprint — diagonal 16.97 — never fitted the `≤ Ø15.57 − 2·t_collar` outline (the Ø25 dome's; `≤ Ø20.37 − 2·t` since the root, 2026-09-30) that the SAME script hands to HW.9 as the board ceiling. ⊕ 2026-09-30: once pos. 19 was bounded (`docs/protocols/hardware/rf_switch_shortlist.md` — tallest candidate ≤ 1.10 mm) the constant was retargeted 2.5 → 2.4 = the antenna `NN02-224`, the module bound retired; no BOM-FR4 row verdict moved (the unsourced FR4 1.0 / B2B 10 contrast row flipped true at exactly 2.4), and the retarget is recorded in the constant's own comment, not only here. The miss cost a founder verdict (chip-down STM32WLE5CC) and it surfaced from a human reading the BOM, not from any model. 🔑 **A member admitted to a budget carries its footprint AND its height, or the verdict it drives is about half the part:** before adding a part to any `*_budget` dict, ask which OTHER envelope of the same script it must also fit. ⚠️ The same blind spot is structural for area, and it bit MY OWN tool (HW.9/HW.37, 2026-09-29): `tools/firmware/board_area_budget.rb` sums COURTYARDS per deck side, and I priced the reflow EDLC `FC0H474` off that sum alone («target Ø23.4») — the founder ratified it on that price. Applying it found the binding axis elsewhere: the part's base is a 16.3 × 16.3 SQUARE, a square on a round board needs its DIAGONAL, and the B2B socket must sit in the segment beside it on the SAME side — true floor Ø ≈ 24.5–26.3, so the verdict went back with the corrected price. The calculator now carries that second axis (`geometric_floor`: EDLC + socket on one side, offset optimised). 🔑 **An area sum is a claim about SMALL parts; a part whose own diagonal is a large fraction of the outline is judged by its geometry, and a price quoted from the sum alone is half a price.**

12. 🔴 **Two scripts that model ONE physical part describe the same hardware only if they cite the same part number — each can agree with its own document while the pair describes two different parts** (`00_07` HW.43, 2026-09-10). The pogo pin had three homes that diverged on three axes at once: the BOM said `Mill-Max 0906`, the spec `0908/0909` with an SS 316 spring, `59_contact_endurance_check` modelled BeCu C17200 with that alloy's S-N anchors, and `52_z_stack_tolerance` took a travel attributed to 0908/0909. No gate saw it, because every surface was locally consistent — the divergence exists only BETWEEN them, and it surfaced from a hand cross-read of `02_01 §3.1` ↔ `02_02 §2.2`. 🔑 **The material of a spring is not a label, it IS the S-N input,** so a mismatch here moves the fatigue verdict, not the wording. Before a script declares a part's material, travel or rate, grep the other scripts and the spec for the SAME part and name the P/N the value comes from. ⛔ **Do not reconcile the homes one by one without a choice first:** aligning each file to its neighbour cements whichever branch you touched first as if it had been ratified. The verdict HW.43 got (founder 2026-09-18) is instructive — the alloy turned out NOT to be a free variable of a catalogue part at all, so the fork moved to the axis that actually bit (travel), and that only became visible once the choice was put before the edits.

13. 🔴 **A THIRD-PARTY instrument writes "I did not measure this" with the SAME value it writes measurements — and nothing in our tree can see it, because the sentinel lives in that tool's code** (`00_07` HW.5.IS, 2026-10-04; reading script `80_chem11_a3d_crosscheck.py`, committed output `tools/in_silico/data/chem11_a3d/`). Aggrescan3D forces a residue's score to exactly `0.0` whenever its relative solvent accessibility falls below 10 % (`min_surf = 10` plus the branch `if not central.agg_sup == 0` in `aggrescan/aggrescan_3d.py` of release 1.0.2), and writes that into the same `score` column of `A3D.csv`, in the same format, as a real value. In our run that was **253 of 600 residues (42 %), 161 of them hydrophobic by A3D's own signed matrix** — i.e. a buried hydrophobic patch and "nothing here" are indistinguishable downstream. ⚠️ **I fell into it on the FIRST reading of that very run:** aggregating those zeros produced a tidy "no positively-scoring neighbour at Gln258/Gln200", which hid a real (if weak) neighbour and sent a partly-false ground to the founder; the verdict survived only because its force lay elsewhere.

**Practice:** before taking a max, a min, a ranking or a mean over ANY third-party numeric output, open that tool's source and find the value it uses for "not applicable" — then exclude those rows and COUNT them in your cache, so the exclusion is visible to the next reader. Our own sentinel discipline (an explicit "not measured" marker rather than a default zero) does not travel into other people's files; the only carrier is this read. ⊕ The same read answers a second question cheaply: a SIGNED propensity matrix makes some residues negative almost by construction (Gln is −1.1394 in A3D), so "the tool did not flag X" can be a statement about the matrix rather than about the molecule — which is why the recipe's criterion had to be re-aimed from the site onto its NEIGHBOUR before it could be answered at all.

14. 🔴 **A converged SCF certifies a STATIONARY POINT, not the ground state — the Newton rescue in `dft_singlepoint` converges from wherever the failed SCF stopped, and on an open shell that can be a spin-contaminated state the cache records as `converged: true`** (`00_07` HW.5.IS, 2026-10-09; numbers — `PIPELINE_STATUS` §Future, the addendum under «① residue»). The rescue starts from the failed orbitals AND their occupation (PySCF's Newton-solver module says so in a comment: «DO NOT change the initial guess mo_occ, mo_coeff»), so it inherits the wrong state rather than escaping it. ωB97X/6-31G(d) NO₂ Os(III): the first 100 cycles (under the tier's 0.3 shift) ended at `<S²>` 2.21, the rescue «converged» at 1.767 (a doublet is 0.75), and ΔE_red moved 2.76 eV against def2-TZVP, while the same basis change moved the NH₂ control by 22 meV. The excess is ATTRIBUTED to Os(III) — Os(II) is closed-shell RKS, unrescued at both tiers — and that the state is not the ground one is inferred from the transfer, not yet shown; the re-run is the test. 📌 Дописка 2026-10-11 (the sentence above is not rewritten): shown — the re-run from a doublet start converged 0.1016 Ha (2.76 eV) below the rescue's state, at `<S²>` 0.7555, internally stable. The cache carries no `<S²>`, so the only witness was the gitignored log. 🔑 **Two cheap discriminators, both from what is already computed beside the point:** (a) `<S²>` in the run log — PySCF prints it for every UKS solution; (b) the Os(III) HOMO–LUMO gap against its family at the SAME level of theory — flat in both series measured so far (B3LYP ten points within 0.22 eV; ωB97X/def2-TZVP three within 0.20), and the bad point sat 1.9 eV below its same-tier control; a flag on the eV scale, not a threshold. ⚠️ **The artefact can MIMIC a named weakest link:** this one looked exactly like «the basis effect on the nitro group need not be the one on Me/H», the risk its own run verdict had named — so an effect an order of magnitude larger at one point than at its control is an SCF-state question before it is a chemistry one (an instance of the `00_05 §5` norm on closing a prediction leg, verbatim: «назви АДРЕСУ, якою передбачення мало спрацювати, і перевір, чи змінилась саме вона — збіг результату виглядає точнісінько як успіх»).

**Practice:** an open-shell point is accepted by its `<S²>`, never by `converged`; when a series point moves far more than its control, read its log before its chemistry. **Since 2026-10-11 the structural fix is code** (⚖️ founder 2026-10-09): `dft_singlepoint` records `s2` and `rescued` (and, on request, `stable_internal` after an internal stability analysis); `21e` accepts an Os(III) by `s2` ≤ `S2_MAX_DOUBLET` (`tools/in_silico/lib/constants.py`), reuses only accepted states, starts a recomputed Os(III) from its own Os(II) closed shell minus one β electron, and NAMES every Os(III) it compares without `s2` (`os3_s2_not_recorded` — rows written before 2026-10-11, which in the cache stand on `converged` alone: a run log prints `<S²>` for the 21e rows, the centre caches of 21f/21d and the 34b forms have none) instead of passing them silently. ⚠️ **The refusal is 21e's only — the sisters still read `converged`:** `21` · `21b` · `21d` · `21f` · `34` · `34b` · `75` · `76` run Os(III) through the same runner, so their next runs RECORD `s2`, but none refuses on it, and their committed caches carry none; `21g` has its own SCF wrapper with its own Newton rescue and records nothing (`00_05 §4`: a rule ratified for one leg does not reach its sister by itself).

15. 🔴 **A model of how a load TRAVELS between two parts is a list of paths before it is a number — inventory every coupling canon already defines between the two ends, and every load that does not depend on the one you set out to price, before computing any of them** (`00_07` HW.43, script `87`, 2026-10-13). The first `87` priced the board stack's inertia as «the» transfer of trunk sway to the pogo pins and called the capsule a rigid appendage; three adversarial rounds then found, each time, a path or a coupling the canon already held: the static axial share of the weight at the 10° install angle (it lowers the mean, so «≤ m·a/F» stopped being a bound); the capsule's own WEIGHT as the largest lateral load on the rim (the rigid-carry premise turned into a condition on an O-ring force canon lacks); a mass-independent kinematic path — the Poisson part of bending strain moving the bus-wire tip — that the inertial framing could not see; and, priced in its first form, that path ignored the liner's grip and the Zone 1↔2 press-fit `01_01 §1.4` already ties the tip and the anode to the flange with, so it reaches the pin only if both slip. A requirement sized «just enough at end of life» (`52`'s rim clamp) was also read as a margin. 🔑 **Before the first line of a transfer model: (a) draw the chain end to end and, for every joint, name what canon says holds it (press-fit · grip · clamp · weld · friction pair and its µ) — a joint you cannot name is the first unknown, not a rigid link; (b) list the loads by what they scale with (mass · displacement · strain · temperature), so a path independent of your variable is visible; (c) for every «≤», say what it is a bound on and with which sign the static terms enter; (d) a requirement written to hold at a margin gives zero margin at that end.** The same discipline that keeps a cited constant in its role (#10) keeps a computed path in its place in the chain. ⊕ **In a joint where TWO members relax — the clamp and the seal — the requirement is a maximum over time of their ratio, not the unrelaxed load over the relaxed clamp** (`88` · `87`, 2026-10-13, both caught by an adversary): pairing the seal's initial reaction with the clamp's 20-year fraction is one corner, so print both ends (at assembly ⊥ the seal never relaxing) and the share the seal must LOSE for the margin to hold; and before a quantity takes a name like «needed at the end of life», check whether the algebra makes it an INITIAL value — `87`'s did, and a «≥ 26 % retained» requirement was built on the misname. ⊕ **Two more, from the second pass on the same pair:** (1) a bound that switches between STATES of a joint (seated ⊥ lifted, stuck ⊥ slipped) is a bound only if the switch can go BACK — `87`'s «normal ≥ max(C, L)» assumed it, but a rim lifted onto a creeping ledge does not reseat (`52`), so after a lift the bound is L alone, and «whatever the ring retains» stopped being a GUARANTEE for every clamp below `52`'s printed form (whether such a clamp lifts at all depends on how the two relaxations compare — the third pass found a case where it does not; taking the lift as not undone is then a conservative assumption, name it so); (2) in a deflection-controlled clamp of two materials, bound the thermal sensitivity before quoting any margin — in the STIFFEST case (the compressed member is the loop's only compliance and spans the whole loop) it is E·A·Δα per kelvin, free of the preload and of the length, here ≈ 29–55 N/K (A at the contact ⊥ the column section — annuli, not the lug contacts) against a 157 N clamp; series compliance only lowers it, so it is a ceiling — and the largest series term may be a small CONTACT (three lugs on a ledge), not the column, so the ceiling alone never decides whether the loop needs a spring; (3) in a DEFLECTION-controlled clamp the interference TOLERANCE is a displacement path through the same k, and it usually dwarfs the thermal one (`89`, 2026-10-13: ±0.05 mm against ≈ 12.6 µm of interference-equivalent temperature — 89 % of the spread; an adversary found it, my inventory had only temperature); and a relaxation fraction applies only as far as the relaxing member IS the loop's compliance — the k → 0 end of a «relaxing-PEEK» form is not a loop at all, its real limit is force control, so a number read at that end (the ratified 0.28 mm) is the stiff loop's LOWER BOUND and one point on a curve of an unmeasured k — and the series formula f_eff = f/(f + φ(1 − f)) shows the curve, while my first wording «reached by no design» overclaimed; and the SIGN comes from the loop: a low-CTE body clamping a high-CTE one over the same span unloads in the cold and loads in the heat. Every margin before that check is a statement about the assembly temperature.

16. 🔴 **A reduced-cofactor SMILES is a TAUTOMER claim, and the formula cannot check it — before a cascade runs on it, verify WHERE the hydrogens sit against the species the text names.** (`00_07` HW.5.IS, self-review round 1, cluster 4, 2026-10-15.) 17. 🔴 **A coupling taken as half the difference of two SCF solutions is a coupling only if the two states are CHARGE-LOCALISED and their difference clears the SCF tolerance — both halves fail silently, and the rate they feed is squared in the first and meaningless in the second.** (`00_07` HW.5.IS, Стаття 1 self-review round 1, cluster 8, 2026-10-15; script `24_dft_hopping_integrals.py`, consumer `25`.) `24`'s docstring promised «initial guess biased toward electron localization on each metal center». Three years of consumers read it as t_ij; the paper reported a Marcus rate ∝ t², a bottleneck identity and a design verdict on it. The two-state orbital diabatisation of `24b` on the same cluster returns 4.3× more (0.00546 eV) and, with it, a site-energy gap the crude number assumed away — moving the published margin ×18.
   **Check (minutes, no new DFT):** (1) read the code that prepares state B, not the docstring — a localised state needs a constraint, a biased density matrix or a fragment guess, and `level_shift` is none of those; (2) divide the energy difference by the `conv_tol` both runs used and print the ratio INTO the cache beside t_ij (`dscf_coupling_resolution` in `25`); (3) if the ratio is single-digit, the number is noise with units — use a diabatisation and keep the old table as a named record (`scenarios_crude_dscf`), never as a second reading. ⚠️ A tolerance ratio is necessary, not sufficient: the Co–Ce and Ce–C differences clear it by 50× and 830× and are still not diabatic couplings — the locality half stands on its own.

18. 🔴 **A converged GEOMETRY certifies a STATIONARY POINT, not a minimum — and a symmetric start has no gradient out of its symmetry plane, so it converges to the symmetric stationary point, which only a Hessian classifies.** (`00_07` HW.5.IS, 2026-10-16; script `32b`, run 1 kept in the gitignored `cache/runs/32b/run1_2026-10-16/`.) The geometric twin of #14. `EmbedMolecule(randomSeed=42)` + MMFF94s gives 1,5-dihydrolumiflavin EXACTLY planar (heavy atoms 0.000 Å out of plane, every N–H in the plane), and geomeTRIC at B3LYP/6-31G(d)+C-PCM converged from it in 11 steps (`grad_max` 8.8e-5) to the planar saddle of the butterfly: 255i cm⁻¹, 94 % on the N5–H hydrogen out of plane, and 66i on the N10-methyl rotor. `converged: true`, tautomer check passed — only `32b`'s Hessian gate (a mode beyond `IMAG_TOL_CM`) refused it. The well is shallow: a rigid cut along the 255i mode gave −1.05 meV at 0.1 Å and +10.6 at 0.2 Å, so the default gmax (4.5e-4 Ha/bohr) is met within a step of the saddle — an escape needs TIGHT gradients, not a bigger step (0.2 Å is already uphill on that cut).
   **Practice:** a thermochemistry or a «relaxed» energy is accepted only with every mode real (within a DECLARED tolerance); a mode beyond it is stepped off along (`32b` `escape_geometry`: summed modes scaled to one largest displacement — scaled one by one, two modes sharing an atom move it further), re-optimised on TIGHT gradients with the DEFAULT displacement criteria (TIGHT's own dmax holds methyl rotors to a 4e-6 Å trust radius) and a tighter SCF, and the saddle stays in the record with its height over the minimum. A start built from SMILES is symmetric whenever the molecule's conjugated core is — ask before any optimisation of a reduced or saturated ring. ⚠️ **The sisters optimise from the same planar start without a Hessian:** `21g` (FADH₂ and its cation, B3LYP/def2-SVP), `33` and `29b` — what that does to their numbers is the open leg of `00_07` HW.5.IS, priced by `32b`'s `saddle_above_minimum_meV`; and no relaxed Os form of `21e` has a Hessian either (`00_05 §4`: a rule ratified for one leg does not reach its sister by itself).
