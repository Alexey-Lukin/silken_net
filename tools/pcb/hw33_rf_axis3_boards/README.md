# HW.33 — test boards of the RF mock-up, axis 3 (an antenna booster on a ground of our contour)

Axis 3 measures the efficiency of an antenna booster on a ground the size of the node's board — the number the ruling «for the small ground we pay in efficiency» stands on, and the one no evaluation board measures by construction ([`02_01 §5.3`](../../../docs/02_01_Hardware_Architecture_and_BOM.md); procedure — [`rf_mockup_bench_sheet`](../../../docs/protocols/hardware/rf_mockup_bench_sheet.md) §5). The sheet asks for two diameters — the sentinel Ø20.37 and Ø21.87 (in place of Ø19.37 since 2026-10-15: the root package of `02_01 §3.5` puts every board target with the clearance segment as a keep-out at or above Ø20.57) — and for both boosters that fit the contour where possible, so there are four boards. State and the order leg: [`00_07`](../../../docs/00_07_Action_Plan_Tracker.md) HW.33.

## What is here

| File | Role |
|---|---|
| `build_boards.py` | the source — generates everything below (AGPL-3.0-or-later, like all tooling); its header carries the vendor's footprint and evaluation-board numbers with the page each was read on, and every layout choice that is ours |
| `hw33_axis3_<booster>_d<Ø>.kicad_pcb` · `.kicad_pro` | the four boards and their design rules (KiCad 10) — `nn02224`/`nn02201` × `d20p37`/`d21p87` |
| `fab/<board>/gerbers/` | Gerber layers + Excellon drill, as text |

The design outputs are hardware design and fall under **CERN-OHL-S-2.0** by the zone map in [`/NOTICE`](../../../NOTICE); KiCad's S-expressions have no comment syntax, so they carry no per-file tag.

Rebuild (KiCad 10 installed in `~/Applications/KiCad`):

```bash
~/Applications/KiCad/KiCad.app/Contents/Frameworks/Python.framework/Versions/3.9/bin/python3.9 -B \
```

The run refuses a placement whose pads or booster body leave the contour, or a part that reaches into the clearance area; a board that fails DRC (any violation or unconnected item) is deleted with its project before any fabrication file is written; it also writes `fab/<board>/<board>_gerbers.zip` for the upload (ignored by git — it is rebuilt from the text gerbers).

## The boards

- **A disc of the contour, 2 layers, FR4 1.6 mm, ground poured on both layers and stitched.** The disc is the radiator: a booster excites the ground it sits on.
- **The booster at the top edge, in a clearance segment only 1.0 mm deeper than its pads.** The vendor's evaluation boards give the booster an 11 mm strip; ours is the narrowest the footprint allows, on purpose — the node's board budget has no room for a wide one, so the board measures what the product can have, and the number comes out more pessimistic than a wide clearance would give. Narrowest is not small: the segment over the chord is ≈ 26–30 % of the disc for `NN02-224` and ≈ 16–18 % for `NN02-201` (Ø21.87 · Ø20.37) — every run prints it in mm². The width is ratified (founder, 2026-10-12): the narrowest — grounds, price and weakest link in the bench sheet §5. Changing the strip is one constant in the generator, not a new design.
- **The feed:** a grounded coplanar line ≈ 51 Ω, then four 0603 sites — A shunt · B series · C shunt · D series (D fitted with 0 Ω) — and a U.FL. The VNA starts from the values of the smallest published ground for `NN02-224`, 40 × 20 — 27 nH · 8.2 nH · 2.0 pF ([`02_01 §5.3`](../../../docs/02_01_Hardware_Architecture_and_BOM.md)) — whose topology this pass did not see; the board takes a π (A B C) as well as a T (B C D). The vendor's ISM application note has six networks in our band, all read off their schematics: two L on 131 × 60 (863–870 MHz: 30 nH series + 20 nH shunt — B + C) and four T, among them the CR80 board's 863–870 MHz one (30 nH series · 13 nH shunt · 3 nH series — B · C · D) on a 75 × 54 ground, the smallest seen; not one is a π. The vendor's values are a starting point for the VNA, never ours.
- **Stitching:** a via 1.2 mm from each shunt's ground pad, one 1.2 mm from a ground pad of the U.FL (its other ground pad, against the clearance or the edge, has its nearest via 3.2–4.2 mm away on all four boards), a grid over the ground, and a fence along the line where the parts' pads leave room — at 868 MHz (λ_g ≈ 208 mm) every one of those spacings is far inside λ/20.
- **The mounting pad of the booster is left unconnected** — both manuals say it must not be grounded. The booster's body is placed as well as its pads: the `NN02-201` body (7.0 × 3.0) is wider than its pads, so it is the body that meets the contour.

## Ordering notes (👤)

1. **Order the boards bare** — nothing here needs a PCBA house: every matching part is chosen on the VNA, and the booster's two pads hand-solder.
2. **Parts:** the boosters `NN02-224` and `NN02-201` (from a distributor of Ignion), Hirose `U.FL-R-SMT-1`, a U.FL → SMA(F) **bulkhead** pigtail in **two lengths**, and a tuning set around the vendor's values in the parts the vendor itself names — Murata LQW18AN (0603) inductors, LQW15AN (0402) for the smallest ones, GJM1555 (0402) capacitors; a 0402 part sits on the 0603 sites by hand. Two lengths, because check (в) of the bench sheet's §5 (i) changes the cable length inside the cap, and a bulkhead pigtail lies wholly inside it, so one length cannot be changed; best of the same cable and connector, so that the check moves the length alone. The window puts the wall 86–105 mm from the DUT's centre and the U.FL (J1) sits 6.0–7.3 mm off that centre, so a pigtail of about 100 mm reaches the wall with slack only in the lower part of the window — the cap therefore belongs in the lower part of that window (a machine consequence; the cap's own window, inner Ø 172–210 mm, does not name it). The pair is TE Linx `CSH-SGFB-100-UFFR` (100 mm, two — one spare) and Taoglas `CAB.720` (200 mm overall) (⚖️ founder 2026-11-16, [`00_07`](../../../docs/00_07_Action_Plan_Tracker.md) HW.33 `Стан`); their cards give both a Ø1.32 cable, a U.FL plug and an SMA(F) bulkhead jack, so each swap remounts the bulkhead in the cap wall; the alternative named with it — a SMA F-F bulkhead in the wall with swappable cables — was not taken, nor was Taoglas `CAB.011` (IPEX MHF I, 95 mm of Ø1.13 cable). The set, as values (2026-10-18; five of each — four boards, swaps and hand-soldering losses):
   - inductors, LQW15AN (0402): 1.3 · 1.5 · 1.8 · 2.2 · 2.4 · 2.7 · 3.0 · 3.3 nH — Murata's part search (2026-11-16) finds no LQW15AN at 1.0 or 1.2 nH in any status, discontinued included, so by the rule below both became their nearest neighbour, 1.3, and the set lost one value; 1.8 nH is not in LQW15AN_80 and the search finds it in LQW15AN_00 (`LQW15AN1N8C00D`; its `…0Z` twins are the automotive grade), the rest in LQW15AN_80;
   - inductors, LQW18AN (0603): 3.9 · 4.7 · 5.6 · 6.8 · 7.5 · 8.2 · 8.7 · 10 · 12 · 13 · 15 · 18 · 20 · 22 · 24 · 27 · 30 · 33 · 39 · 47 · 56 · 68 nH;
   - capacitors, GJM1555 C0G (0402): 0.5 · 0.8 · 1.0 · 1.2 · 1.5 · 1.8 · 2.0 · 2.2 · 2.4 · 2.7 · 3.3 · 3.9 · 4.7 · 5.6 · 6.8 · 8.2 · 10 pF.

   Every value of the six application-note networks in the header of `build_boards.py`, filled out with E12 steps and two sub-1 pF trimmers (0.5 · 0.8 pF, not E12). The top of the inductor range is insurance, not a trend: our ground is smaller than every ground the vendor published a network for, and the vendor's own in-band series values do not grow as the ground shrinks (30 · 30 · 27 nH from 131 × 60 to 40 × 20), so the kit simply doubles the largest. Whether each value exists in its family is checked in Murata's own catalogue, with the vendor's own part numbers as positive controls — `LQW18AN30NG00` · `LQW18AN20NG00` · `LQW18AN13NG00` · `LQW15AN3N0B80D`; a value the family lacks (the smallest LQW15AN values are the likeliest) is replaced by its nearest neighbour. Checked 2026-11-16 against Murata's reference specifications `JELF243A-0024` (LQW18AN_00) and `JELF243A-0100` (LQW15AN_80) and its part search: every LQW18AN and GJM1555C1H value of the set exists (the LQW15AN gaps are in the row above). Below 10 nH the LQW18AN tolerance is a letter in nH — C ±0.2 or D ±0.5, and 4.7 nH comes only as D — so those order lines end `…C00D` / `…D00D`, never `…G00D`; in LQW15AN_80 the values up to 1.7 nH come only as C or D as well. Whether an order line is a part currently in production is read at order time.
3. **Clip ferrite chokes on the feeding cable** — on a ground of ≈ λ/16–17 (Ø21.87 · Ø20.37 against λ ≈ 345 mm at 868 MHz) the cable becomes part of the radiator, and without chokes the reading is optimistic (bench sheet §5). The Würth `74271111` named for this is rated for a 3.5–5 mm cable, so on the 1.32 mm coax it slides; no ready choke for a ≈ 1.3 mm cable with a vendor curve at 868 MHz turned up in one web search (2026-11-16), and a slip-on choke would have to pass the U.FL plug (the pigtail has connectors at both ends), so the clip stays and is held by tape stops on both sides — how, where, and the bench check of whether one pass is enough or two turns are needed, is step 3 of the bench sheet [`rf_mockup_bench_sheet`](../../../docs/protocols/hardware/rf_mockup_bench_sheet.md) §6 (the purchase verdict of 2026-11-16 did not judge the clip; [`00_07`](../../../docs/00_07_Action_Plan_Tracker.md) HW.33 `Стан`); it reopens only with Würth's safety key `74271`. Its own sheet rates it at 25 and 100 MHz only; read by eye off the steep fall of its typical curve (one turn), it gives roughly 290–350 Ω at 868 MHz — typical, not guaranteed.
4. The instruments of axis 3 — VNA, a closed Wheeler cap sized inside the window of the bench sheet's §5 (i) (not «≥ 55 mm and the bigger the safer»: a large cap is a cavity that resonates near our band), a step attenuator — are in the bench's instrument list; without them the boards wait.

> **Ordering codes — the suffix is the packaging, in the manufacturers' own sheets (2026-11-16):** `U.FL-R-SMT-1` is sold only with a packaging suffix — `U.FL-R-SMT-1(10)`, which Hirose's drawing of that part (`EDC3-302540-10`, marked «for reference», revision 2010) gives as a reel of 2,500 and distributors sell cut from it; `LQW18AN30NG00` is sold as `LQW18AN30NG00D`, where Murata's page gives the last letter as the package specification code and its reference specification `JELF243A-0024` reads D as taping (4,000 a reel) and B as bulk. The part numbers above stay as the design's; the suffix is the order line.
