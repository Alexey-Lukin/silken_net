## 1. Thesis (honest, one paragraph)

We compute the first-principles electron-transfer energetics of the Gen 2.0 EBFC anode→mediator→cathode chain. The **PCET redox potential of the flavin** is computed with the standard thermodynamic proton reference — 65 mV from experiment at a screening level that omits the flavin's zero-point energy, ≈210 mV more negative with relaxed geometries and that term, so no level validates it; the **direct-electron-transfer kinetics through a Cu–Co node of the Co–Cu–Ce ZIF cathode**, computed with Nelsen reorganization energies, expose a **λ-sensitive, DET margin conditional on λ(Co)** and a low-λ-metal lever, computed indicatively (the earlier large margin was a geometry+λ artifact); a **Hammett trend** for the Os(III/II) mediator agrees in direction with three measured series and overshoots on the acceptor side (its slope is method-dependent, §3.3), consistent with the trade-off behind the experimental optimum, which it does not locate; and the **anode→mediator cascade, compared like for like** (the two-electron, two-proton flavin couple against each one-electron osmium couple on one SHE scale), is downhill in every osmium form at every level of the flavin treatment within one protocol — which omits spin–orbit coupling on osmium, which reverses the device couple on one flavin level at the literature size for homoleptic [Os(bpy)₃] (and up to 10 cells at the larger rows of ref 63's Table S2), so the protocol does not determine its sign (⊕ 2026-10-19, `95` `shared_shifts`) — while its magnitude and the split of the remaining gap are method-dependent and its near-exact agreements are cancellations (⚖️ founder 2026-10-16, «direction + table»); explicit-water QM/MM, spin–orbit coupling on osmium and a film model are the next steps toward the magnitude. The contribution is mechanistic insight + a like-for-like methodology lesson — not a re-confirmation of the experimentally-known cascade.
